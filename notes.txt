game loop:
1) you choose hero's name (and maybe choose from some characters with unique stats andmaybe skills)
2) choose the next Stage choosing from 3 options
3) you can Explore the Stage, choosing from three Locations. You can refresh the Locations paying 1 EXP.
4) while exploring a Location you can fight or interact with NPCS. If enemies are ready for an ambush, you have to defeat first.
- Fighting and completing Quests gives you Exp. Exp given is (1 * stage level)
- You can spend (4 * stage level) to unlock the Boss Location, where you will fight to the death. If you win, you get to the next Stage. 
- You can spend 1 exp to level a party element's stat or to flee from a tough fight. 
4b) if your level is below 7, go back to step 2
5) Fight the Dragon in the Mountain. If you beat him, you win the game.
